# Networking outreach

## Message rules
- LinkedIn connection note: under 300 characters. Email/DM: under 120 words.
- Structure: who I am (1 line) -> specific reason I'm writing to THEM -> one small, easy ask -> thanks. Never ask a stranger for a job; ask for 15 minutes, advice, or a referral only after rapport or when they've posted the role.
- Personalize with one verifiable detail (their post, team, shared school); if you can't find one, say so and keep it short.
- Referral ask: make it easy - include the job link, a 2-line fit summary, and permission to decline.

## Cadence
Follow up once after 5-7 days, once more after 10-14, then stop. After an interview: thank-you within 24 hours. Log touches in `job-tracker`.

## Rubric
- [ ] Within length limit (count characters for LinkedIn notes)
- [ ] One clear ask, easy to say yes or no to
- [ ] Specific to the recipient; not a template blast
- [ ] No exaggeration, no pressure, no mention of other offers unless true and relevant
- [ ] User's current employer is not criticized

## Rules
The user sends the messages themselves; draft only. Don't scrape or automate bulk messaging (violates LinkedIn terms and reads as spam).
